WHY MODERN SECURITY PREFERS PASSKEYS (FIDO2 / WEBAUTHN)

Traditional passwords require transmitting a shared secret to a remote database—vulnerable to credential stuffing, data breaches, and phishing. Passkeys use asymmetric public-key cryptography bound to your device hardware, making remote credential theft mathematically impossible.
