🎣 Fake Email & Scam Spotter

Inspect real-life scam emails and learn how to catch the 3 hidden traps scammers use.

Inbox: Suspicious Message
Today, 09:15 AM
From: Google Security Team <support@accounts.gооgle.com>
Subject: URGENT: Your account will be locked in 60 minutes!

Dear User,

We detected an unauthorized sign-in attempt to your account from Russia. If this was not you, you must verify your identity immediately.

⚠️ Warning: If you do not click below within 60 minutes, your email, photos, and files will be permanently deleted!

Click Here to Verify & Unlock Account Real Link: http://google-verify-login.xyz/steal-password

3 Red Flags Revealed:

  • 1. Fake Cyrillic Letter in Address: The sender email looks like `google.com`, but uses a Russian Cyrillic letter 'о'. It leads to a scammer website!
  • 2. Artificial Urgency ("60 Minutes!"): Scammers always create panic so you click quickly without thinking.
  • 3. Deceptive Link Target: The button says "Google", but the actual link destination is a shady `.xyz` domain!
