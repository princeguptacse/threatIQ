# 🛡️ threatIQ — Personal Digital Safety & Privacy Hub

---

## 🎯 What is threatIQ?

Most people don't realize their phone photos leak their exact home address, or that their passwords can be cracked in 2 seconds.

**threatIQ** is an ultra-clean, intuitive, 100% client-side digital safety platform that gives everyday users and judges **simple, practical tools** to protect themselves online — with zero confusing jargon and zero backend data collection.

---

## 🚀 The 4 Core Safety Pillars

1. **🔐 Password Safety & Crack-Time Calculator**
   - Type any password to see real-world crack times (from `< 1 second` to `millions of years`).
   - Generates memorable, uncrackable 4-word passphrases with 1 click.

2. **🕵️ Photo Location & GPS Cleaner (EXIF Scrubber)**
   - Drag and drop any photo from your phone.
   - Instantly exposes hidden GPS coordinates and camera serial tags.
   - **1-Click Clean**: Physically strips all location metadata right in your browser canvas and downloads a safe, sanitized photo.

3. **🎣 Fake Email & Scam Spotter**
   - Inspect realistic phishing attacks (e.g. Google security alert scam).
   - Click to reveal the **3 hidden red flags** scammers use (lookalike Cyrillic characters, false urgency, deceptive links).
   - Earn XP points by flagging scams!

4. **🚨 Live Global Cyber Defense Radar**
   - Clean, animated world map showing simulated cyber attacks getting deflected across global hubs in real time.
   - Interactive shield deflection button.

5. **🏆 Digital Safety Champion Certificate**
   - 3-question quick quiz.
   - Type your name to render and download a personalized, high-resolution certificate (PNG).

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** Semantic HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Modern Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
- **Metadata Sanitization:** HTML5 Canvas offline rasterization + Exif.js
- **Audio:** Web Audio API (synthesized clicks & beeps without external MP3 files)
- **Visuals:** HTML5 Canvas 2D Radar, Lucide Icons, Canvas Confetti
- **Privacy:** 100% Client-Side. No servers, no tracking, zero data leakage.

## 💻 How to Run Locally

Open `index.html` directly in any web browser:
